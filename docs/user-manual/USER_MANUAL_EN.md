# ICTServe User Manual

**Version**: 3.0.0  
**Last Updated**: November 2025  
**System**: ICTServe - MOTAC ICT Service Portal

---

## Introduction

ICTServe is an internal ICT service management system for MOTAC staff. The system allows you to:

- Submit technical support tickets (Helpdesk)
- Apply for ICT asset loans
- Track application status
- Manage user profile

---

## Section 1: System Access

### 1.1 Login

1. Visit **<https://ictserve.motac.gov.my>**
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password**
3. Click the **Login** button
4. The system will redirect you to the Dashboard

**Note**: If you forgot your password, click the "Forgot Password" link on the login page.

### 1.2 Change Language

- Click the language icon at the top right of the screen
- Select **Bahasa Melayu** or **English**
- Your language preference will be saved for future sessions

### 1.3 Logout

1. Click your name at the top right
2. Select **Logout**
3. You will be returned to the login page

## Section 3: Technical Support Tickets (Helpdesk)

### 3.1 Submit New Ticket

#### Step 1: Basic Information

1. Click **New Ticket** from Dashboard
2. Select problem **Category**:
   - Hardware
   - Software
   - Network
   - Email
   - Others
3. Enter a brief **Title** for the problem
4. Click **Next**

#### Step 2: Problem Details

1. Describe the problem in detail in the **Description** field
2. Select your **Division** from the list
3. Enter your **Location** (e.g., Level 5, Room 501)
4. Click **Next**

#### Step 3: Attachments (Optional)

1. Drag and drop files to the upload area, OR
2. Click to select files from your computer
3. Maximum 5 files, maximum size 10MB each
4. Supported formats: JPG, PNG, PDF, DOC, DOCX
5. Click **Next**

#### Step 4: Confirmation

1. Review all entered information
2. Check the **Declaration** box:
   > "I hereby declare and confirm that all information provided in this Damage Report eForm is true and accurate."
3. Click **Submit**

#### After Submission

- You will receive a **Ticket Number** (e.g., HD2025000001)
- Confirmation email will be sent within 60 seconds
- Use the ticket number to track status

### 3.2 Track Ticket Status

1. Go to **Application History** from the menu
2. Search for ticket using ticket number or title
3. Ticket statuses:
   - **New**: Ticket newly received
   - **In Progress**: Being handled by ICT staff
   - **Awaiting Feedback**: Requires additional information from you
   - **Resolved**: Problem has been solved
   - **Closed**: Ticket has been closed

### 3.3 Claim Guest Tickets

If you previously submitted tickets as a guest (without logging in):

1. Go to Dashboard
2. View the **Claimable** card
3. Click **Claim Submissions**
4. Select tickets to claim
5. Enter the **OTP** sent to your email
6. Tickets will be linked to your account

## Section 4: ICT Asset Loans

### 4.1 Apply for New Loan

#### Step 1: Select Asset

1. Click **New Loan** from Dashboard
2. Select the type of asset needed:
   - Projector
   - Laptop
   - Camera
   - Audio Visual Equipment
3. Check asset availability
4. Click **Next**

#### Step 2: Loan Dates

1. Select **Pickup Date**
   - Minimum 3 working days from application date
   - Excludes weekends and public holidays
2. Select **Return Date**
   - Maximum 14 days from pickup date
3. Click **Next**

#### Step 3: Loan Purpose

1. Enter the **Purpose** of the loan
2. Specify the **Usage Location**
3. If applying on behalf of someone else:
   - Check **On Behalf**
   - Enter Responsible Officer details
4. Click **Next**

#### Step 4: Terms and Conditions

1. Read all 11 loan conditions in the accordion
2. Check the agreement box
3. Click **Submit Application**

#### After Submission

- Application will be sent to Approving Officer (Grade 41+)
- You will receive a confirmation email
- Status can be tracked on Dashboard

### 4.2 Approval Process

1. Approving Officer will receive an email
2. Approval can be done via:
   - Link in email (without login)
   - ICTServe Portal
3. You will be notified via email about the decision

### 4.3 Asset Pickup

After application is approved:

1. You will receive a **4-digit OTP** via email
2. Visit the Information Management Division on the pickup date
3. Provide the OTP to staff
4. Staff will verify and hand over the asset
5. Keep the pickup receipt

### 4.4 Asset Return

1. Return the asset on or before the return date
2. Staff will inspect the asset condition
3. If damaged, a Helpdesk ticket will be created automatically
4. You will receive return confirmation via email

## Section 5: User Profile

### 5.1 View Profile

1. Click your name at the top right
2. Select **Profile**
3. Information displayed:
   - Name
   - Email (read-only)
   - Staff ID (read-only)
   - Grade (read-only)
   - Division (read-only)

### 5.2 Update Information

#### Editable Information

- Phone number
- Language preference
- Notification settings

#### Read-only Information

- Email, Staff ID, Grade, Division

### 5.3 Request Data Correction

If read-only information is incorrect:

1. Click **Request Correction** next to the field
2. A Helpdesk ticket will be created automatically
3. ICT staff will contact you
4. Correction will be made by Administrator

## Section 6: Approvals (Grade 41+)

### 6.1 Pending Approval List

1. Go to **Approvals** from the menu
2. View list of applications requiring action
3. Information displayed:
   - Applicant name
   - Division
   - Asset type
   - Application date

### 6.2 Approval Process

1. Click on an application to view details
2. Review application information
3. Select action:
   - **Approve**: Application approved
   - **Reject**: Application rejected (enter reason)
4. Applicant will be notified via email

### 6.3 Bulk Approval

1. Check boxes next to applications
2. Click **Approve All** or **Reject All**
3. Confirm action

## Section 7: Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1: What if I forgot my password?
**A**: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page. A reset link will be sent to your email.

### Q2: How long does it take to process a ticket?
**A**: Normal tickets are processed within 1-3 working days. Critical tickets are processed within 24 hours.

### Q3: Can I cancel a loan application?
**A**: Yes, if the application has not been approved. Go to Application History and click "Cancel".

### Q4: What happens if an asset is damaged?
**A**: A Helpdesk ticket will be created automatically. You may be charged replacement costs.

### Q5: How do I contact support?
**A**: Submit a Helpdesk ticket or contact the Information Management Division at ext. 1234.
